Methodology: monthly total returns from auto-adjusted closes (splits & dividends reinvested). Cashflows happen at month-end — contributions buy at target weights, withdrawals sell pro-rata (step-ups each January; a fixed withdrawal that exhausts the balance marks the portfolio depleted); rebalancing happens on calendar boundaries (Dec for annual, quarter-ends for quarterly). CAGR, volatility, drawdowns, Sharpe and Sortino are computed on time-weighted monthly returns, so contributions don't distort them. Sharpe = mean monthly excess return over 3-month T-bills ÷ its standard deviation, annualized ×√12; Sortino uses downside deviation of the same excess returns. Beta/alpha/R² are a CAPM regression of monthly excess returns on the benchmark's (alpha annualized); tracking error = annualized st.dev. of monthly active returns, and information ratio = annualized mean active return ÷ tracking error. Max drawdown is month-end based (intra-month dips are invisible); longest underwater is the peak-to-recovery span of the longest drawdown episode. Calmar = CAGR ÷ |max drawdown|; Ulcer index = RMS of the monthly drawdown series; Martin ratio = annualized mean excess return ÷ Ulcer index. Educational tool — not investment advice.
