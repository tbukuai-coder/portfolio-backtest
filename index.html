Methodology: monthly total returns from auto-adjusted closes (splits & dividends reinvested). Cashflows happen at month-end — contributions buy at target weights, withdrawals sell pro-rata (step-ups each January; a fixed withdrawal that exhausts the balance marks the portfolio depleted); rebalancing happens on calendar boundaries (Dec for annual, quarter-ends for quarterly). CAGR, volatility, drawdowns, Sharpe and Sortino are computed on time-weighted monthly returns, so contributions don't distort them. With a cashflow mode active, the money-weighted return (IRR) is also shown: the discount rate that prices the initial amount, every monthly cashflow, and the final balance — the return the investor's actual dollars earned (with no cashflows it equals CAGR). Sharpe = mean monthly excess return over 3-month T-bills ÷ its standard deviation, annualized ×√12 (the rolling Sharpe view applies the same formula per trailing window); Sortino uses downside deviation of the same excess returns. Beta/alpha/R² are a CAPM regression of monthly excess returns on the benchmark's (alpha annualized); tracking error = annualized st.dev. of monthly active returns, and information ratio = annualized mean active return ÷ tracking error. Up (down) capture = the portfolio's average return across the benchmark's up (down) months ÷ the benchmark's own average in those months. Max drawdown is month-end based (intra-month dips are invisible); longest underwater is the peak-to-recovery span of the longest drawdown episode. Calmar = CAGR ÷ |max drawdown|; Ulcer index = RMS of the monthly drawdown series; Martin ratio = annualized mean excess return ÷ Ulcer index. The return-distribution card's skew is adjusted Fisher–Pearson sample skewness of the monthly returns (negative = left tail, i.e. crash months reach further than rally months).
